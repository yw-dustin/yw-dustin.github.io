I am an M.Sc. student in **Mechatronics, Robotics and Biomechanical Engineering** at the **Technical University of Munich (TUM)**. I received my B.Eng. (Hons) in Aerospace Engineering from the University of Nottingham.

🔬 **Research interests.** I am interested in **learning dexterous manipulation from human videos**, a promising way around the data bottleneck of robot teleoperation. At CAICT, I helped draft an industry standard on evaluating factory robots and tested material-handling robots in live deployments, where I saw how brittle today's systems are outside the conditions they were tuned for. My hands-on work on multi-axis kinematics also draws me to a key open problem: transferring human hand motion to robot hands with different embodiments.
